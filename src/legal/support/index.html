I forgot my passcode

On the lock screen, tap Forgot passcode?. If you added a recovery email, SafeBox emails you a one-time code so you can set a new passcode. If you created a Recovery Key, you can use it instead.

Your passcode is never sent anywhere, so without a recovery email or a Recovery Key it can’t be reset, and we can’t open your vault for you. Add one today in Settings.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

  1. On your old device, open Settings › Backup & Sync and tap Export Backup. SafeBox asks for your Recovery Key (or creates one and shows it once: write it down) and saves an encrypted .safebox file to Files.
  2. Move that file to the new device, for example with AirDrop or iCloud Drive.
  3. Install SafeBox on the new device, set a passcode, then open Settings › Backup & Sync, tap Restore from Backup, pick the file and enter the Recovery Key.

The backup file is free. Restoring only adds what’s missing; it never replaces or deletes anything already in SafeBox.

Will deleting SafeBox delete my files?

Yes. Your vault lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes what’s in it. Export a backup first (see above) if you might want it back.

I updated from an earlier version. Is everything still there?

Yes. Unlock with your usual passcode and your albums, folders, notes and files are all there. After the first unlock, SafeBox encrypts your existing files in the background, one at a time; you’ll see the progress on the Home screen’s storage card. Keep the app open for a while if you have a lot of files.

Restore a purchase or manage SafeBox Plus

Open the SafeBox Plus screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a SafeBox account (there isn’t one).

To change or cancel your plan, use Manage Subscription in the app, or the subscriptions page of your Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is the decoy vault?

With SafeBox Plus you can set a second passcode that opens a separate vault with its own content. Nothing in the app shows that it exists.

Does SafeBox show ads or upload my files?

No ads, ever. Your photos and files stay on your device, and there is no SafeBox server that stores them. The Privacy Policy lists the little that does leave your device.
