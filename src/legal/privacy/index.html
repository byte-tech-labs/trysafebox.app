Who we are

SafeBox (formerly Photo Vault – PixBox) is made by Byte Tech Labs (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the SafeBox app on iPhone and iPad. Using SafeBox is also subject to our Terms of Use.

SafeBox does not require an account. We don’t ask for your name, phone number or any profile, and the name you may enter during setup stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to SafeBox is stored on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Photos, videos, audio, documents, notes, cards and IDs, and the names of your albums, folders and items. Their names and contents are sealed with encryption keys that are derived from your passcode and kept on your device.
  • Your passcode. It never leaves your device, is never sent to us and is never synced, not even to your own iCloud.
  • Break-in photos taken with the front camera after a wrong passcode, and the contents of the decoy vault.
  • Your settings, recently opened items, search and Trash.

Features such as document scanning and reading text from cards and passports run entirely on your device.

We can’t see your vault We have no copy of your content and no way to open it. That also means we can’t recover it for you, so keep your passcode and recovery email up to date.

What leaves your device

Only the following information is ever sent off your device, and only for the purpose described:

Recovery email
If you add a recovery email, SafeBox sends that address and a one-time six-digit code to our email service, which emails the code to you. This happens only when you confirm the address or reset a forgotten passcode. Your passcode and vault content are never included. The email is sent from our Gmail account, so Google processes it and a copy of the sent message remains in that mailbox. To prevent abuse, the service keeps a counter tied to a one-way hash of your address for one hour, and stores nothing else.
Purchases
SafeBox Plus is bought through Apple. To check whether you have SafeBox Plus, SafeBox uses RevenueCat with a random, anonymous ID created by the app. RevenueCat receives your purchase and receipt details from Apple, along with basic technical information such as app version, device model and country. We never link this ID to your name, email or any other identifier.
Usage statistics (you can turn this off)
To learn which parts of SafeBox are used and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: which screen was opened, which button was tapped, and whether an action such as an import or a purchase finished. Events contain only fixed words and counts, for example “3 photos imported”. They never contain your files, names, notes, card details, searches, email address or passcode. Google receives them with a random installation ID created by the app, and basic technical information such as app version, device model, system version, language and country. We don’t set a user ID, we don’t collect the advertising identifier, and the data isn’t used for advertising. Turn it off at any time in Settings › About › Share usage data.
iCloud Sync and backup (optional)
If you turn on iCloud Sync, your content is encrypted on your device and stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account. Only that encrypted data leaves your device. We cannot access it, and Apple cannot read the sealed content. Encrypted backup files go only where you choose to save them.
Feedback
If you send feedback from Settings › Send Feedback, your mail app sends us the message, your email address and the app version shown in the subject. We use it only to reply and improve SafeBox.

When you export or share an item, it goes where you send it, for example your Photos library, Files or another app. That is your choice and is not sent to us.

Device permissions

SafeBox asks for these permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change them at any time in the Settings app.

Camera
To take photos and videos, scan documents and cards straight into your vault, and to take break-in photos if you turn that on.
Microphone
To record voice memos and the sound of videos you take.
Photos
To move photos and videos you choose into SafeBox, remove the originals when you ask, and save exported items back to your library.
Face ID or Touch ID
To unlock SafeBox. Apple handles biometric data; SafeBox never receives it.

SafeBox does not ask for your location, contacts or calendars.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads, and never will.
  • We don’t use advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and we don’t track you across apps or websites.
  • We don’t put your content, names, searches or anything that identifies you in usage statistics.
  • We don’t collect the advertising identifier or the device’s vendor identifier.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your information for marketing.
  • We don’t run servers that store your content.

If you choose to share crash reports or analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. These don’t contain your vault content.

Third-party services

SafeBox works with a small number of services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple: the App Store, payments, iCloud, and Face ID or Touch ID.
  • RevenueCat: anonymous confirmation of SafeBox Plus purchases.
  • Google: Gmail and Google Apps Script, used only to send recovery codes, and Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous usage statistics.

Links in SafeBox, such as to this page, open websites we don’t control. Their own privacy policies apply.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your vault stays until you delete it. Deleting SafeBox from a device deletes everything stored in it on that device.
  • iCloud data stays in your iCloud until you delete it. Turn off iCloud Sync in Settings and choose to delete your data from iCloud, or remove it in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings.
  • Recovery email: remove or change it in Settings › Recovery. The anti-abuse counter expires after one hour.
  • Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. Ask us and we’ll delete them.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax purposes.

Security

SafeBox seals your content with modern encryption, using keys derived from your passcode and protected by your device. Information sent to our email service, RevenueCat, Google Analytics and iCloud travels over encrypted connections.

When you open, preview or export a file, SafeBox may create a short-lived temporary copy so the system can show it. These copies are deleted when you close the file, lock SafeBox or open it again. If you updated from an earlier version, files from that version are encrypted in the background after your first unlock.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design SafeBox so that your content is never in our hands.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your content stays on your device, you already control it directly in the app.

For anything we may hold, such as a feedback email, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. We don’t sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Children

SafeBox is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a feedback email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SafeBox changes. We’ll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we’ll also let you know in the app.
